About ClickPeQR

ClickPeQR

by KRISHNYANSH ZENOVA PEAKS LTD. • RC-9810296

Why we built ClickPeQR

businesses in Nigeria lose sales every day to slow transfers, failed payments and complicated checkout pages. We built ClickPeQR so any shop owner can receive money in seconds with one simple QR - no machine, no stress, no technical knowledge needed.

Who it is for

Market traders, small shops, restaurants, service providers - and their customers who want to pay fast and safely. If you have a bank account, you can use ClickPeQR.

Our Vision

A Nigeria where every small business - from Lagos to Kano - can accept digital payments as easily as receiving cash.

Our Mission

To give every merchant instant, secure, low-cost payment tools with real-time voice confirmation, and to reward every customer and merchant with points on every transaction.
